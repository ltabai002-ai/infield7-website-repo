What the app does

Everything in One Place.

Eight core features. Nothing you don't need.

Clock in / Clock out
Mark attendance from the office. The app checks you're inside the office area.
Office location check
Clock-in works only within the allowed metres around the saved office location.
Live tracking on shift
After clock-in, location continues in the background until clock-out.
Leave requests
Apply for leave from the phone. Status shows pending, approved, or rejected.
Holidays & history
See company holidays and your past attendance.
MPIN lock
Set a 4-digit MPIN after first login. Required whenever the app reopens.
Admin dashboard
Office staff manage employees, attendance, live map, leaves and holidays from the web.
Update notices
When a new Play Store version is published, an in-app popup shows an Update button.

One system. One source of truth.

Why the app uses location

Location, only for work attendance.

Clear rules. No ads. No marketing. No 24-hour tracking.

  • At clock-in / clock-out — the app checks you're inside the office geofence.
  • During your shift — location runs in the background until clock-out, so the office can see whether you're at the workplace.
  • When you're clocked out — no location is collected. Never for ads or marketing.
  • You can refuse — but then you can't clock in from the office or be tracked on shift.
